      What this version knows — and what it doesn't

      • Availability means absent from every league roster, including IR/taxi. It does not prove a player is an immediate free agent rather than awaiting waivers.
      • Current-week projections are used only when fresh and aligned to the selected week. The longer-term estimate is a bye-aware preseason baseline, not a live rest-of-season forecast.
      • For FAAB, the budget reserve is your choice, not a measured optimum. For rolling waivers, no dollar amounts are invented: current priority is context and add/drop alternatives remain value-based.
      • No inferred hidden bids, calibrated win probabilities, automated transactions, or K/DST projections. No keeper-value premium is priced yet.

Keeper value — which to keep

Add each keeper-eligible player with the round and year you ORIGINALLY drafted him (cost drops one round for each year you keep him). Once a keeper's cost would reach round 2 he goes back into the draft pool. A player keeps his original draft round even if he was dropped and later picked back up — only players never drafted in this league cost R12. A kept waiver player decays too — R12 the first year you keep him, R11 the next. Keeping is optional — the tool recommends only players worth more than their keeper cost.

    Within each position, players are ranked by expert consensus (FantasyPros ECR), the most accurate free ranking a drafter has. Across positions the board is ordered by VORP, so positional scarcity — not raw consensus rank — decides who sits above whom. The model contributes the floor→ceiling bands and that value curve. ADP shows where your league-mates' market takes each player. VORP prices in positional scarcity; tier gaps mark real value cliffs. Bands show season floor (p10) to ceiling (p90) from a Monte-Carlo season simulation — games played sampled from historical availability, weeks linked by a measured correlation; the tick is the simulated median, and VORP is built from that same median. When your tier empties, don't chase. Rookies (marked R) carry draft-capital cohort priors — wide bands on purpose; see the about page.

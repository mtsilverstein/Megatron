What is supported?

Every current-season Sleeper league opens in the weekly/start-sit, waiver and in-season trade pages by its league id; those pages read the league's live settings each time they load. The draft board is only built for registered leagues (Gabagool and FAM on Sleeper, plus the ESPN family board), and the pre-draft trade grader is Gabagool only. ESPN in-season tools are not connected.
